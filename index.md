## Project Showcase

### Multi-Dataset Exploratory Data Analysis & Visualization
**Research Question:** How can different statistical visualization techniques effectively uncover hidden patterns and relationships across diverse real-world datasets?

**Visualization 1: Climate Change Analysis**

*Figure 1: A heatmap visualizing global surface temperature anomalies from 1880 to 2015. The clear transition from blue to red indicates a significant and accelerating warming trend over recent decades.*

**Visualization 2: Socioeconomic Impact Analysis**
<!-- 请将括号里的链接替换为你上传到 data-science-project 仓库的图片 RAW 链接 -->
![Education vs Salary Violin Plot]([替换为小提琴图的图片链接](https://github.com/Shan-Letian/data-science-project/blob/main/salary_violin.png?raw=true))
*Figure 2: A violin plot demonstrating the impact of education on annual salary and weekly working hours. It reveals that higher education levels correlate with significantly higher median salaries and wider salary distributions.*

**Key Finding:** 
By employing various Seaborn plots, this project successfully extracted insights from multiple domains. The heatmap reveals an alarming acceleration in global temperature anomalies post-1990. Simultaneously, the violin plot analysis demonstrates a strong positive correlation between higher education levels and increased annual salaries, though it also highlights a wider variance in working hours among higher-degree holders.

**Limitation:** 
The analysis is primarily exploratory and visual. It does not include rigorous statistical hypothesis testing or predictive modeling to prove causality (e.g., proving that education directly causes higher salaries, rather than confounding variables like age or location).
